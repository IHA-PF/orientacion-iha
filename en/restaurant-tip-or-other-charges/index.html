Restaurant: tip or other charges

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Complain on the spot and ask for a corrected bill.

What the law says

  • Tipping is voluntary (Law 1935 of 2018).
  • They must ask whether you accept it before billing; if it appears on the bill, you can refuse to pay it.
  • The price you pay is the advertised price, taxes included (Law 1480, art. 26).

Where and how to complain

  1. Ask them to remove the tip or the undisclosed charge.
  2. If they refuse, report it to the SIC.

Documents to gather

  • Invoice
  • Photo of the menu with prices
