Your consumer rights in Colombia › Transport

Road accident (SOAT insurance)

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Emergency services cannot refuse to treat you.
  • Claim promptly: deadlines and documents vary.

What the law says

  • SOAT insurance covers road accident victims regardless of fault: medical care, disability, death and transport of the victim.
  • If the vehicle had no SOAT or fled, the claim is filed with ADRES.
  • SOAT does not cover damage to your vehicle or belongings: claim those from the person responsible or their insurance.

Where and how to complain

  1. Call 123 and ask for the police accident report.
  2. Emergency care with the vehicle's SOAT details.
  3. Claim to the SOAT insurer or to ADRES.

Documents to gather

  • Police report
  • Medical records
  • Vehicle and SOAT details
  • ID document
