I booked through an app for a month or more: which rules apply?

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • If there is a problem, tell the platform the same day: its reporting deadlines are usually short.

What the law says

  • Accommodation contracts cover stays of less than 30 days (Law 300 of 1996, art. 79). A stay of 30 days or more is no longer tourist accommodation.
  • If it is urban housing, lease rules may apply (Law 820 of 2003): for example, cash deposits cannot be required (art. 16) and, if you don’t leave, the landlord must go to a judge (General Code of Procedure, art. 384).
  • The platform is still the intermediary: its terms and resolution centre apply, and it must have the host identified so you can claim (Law 1480 of 2011, art. 53).
  • Which rules apply depends on the specific case: a lawyer should review it.

Where and how to complain

  1. Keep the booking and the platform terms in force on the booking date (PDF or screenshots).
  2. Check whether you signed a separate contract with the host.
  3. In a dispute, write to the host and the platform, and ask us before handing back the property.

Documents to gather

  • Booking and payments
  • Platform terms in force on that date
  • Messages with the host
  • Separate contract, if any
