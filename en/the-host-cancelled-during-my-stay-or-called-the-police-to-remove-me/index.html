Your rights and obligations in Colombia › Accommodation and tourism

The host cancelled during my stay or called the police to remove me

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Tell the platform immediately, through its emergency channel.
  • Written complaint to the host and the platform: the same day; the provider has 15 business days to reply.
  • Lawsuit before the SIC: up to one year.

What the law says

  • A stay of less than 30 days is an accommodation contract (Law 300 of 1996, art. 79). It only ends early for non-payment, breach of the house rules or a cause expressly agreed (Commercial Code, art. 1197).
  • If the host removes you without one of those causes, it is a breach: you choose equivalent accommodation at the host’s expense, or a refund or compensation (Law 300 of 1996, art. 63), and you can claim extra costs.
  • During your stay, the accommodation is your home (Constitution, art. 28; Constitutional Court, C-519 of 2007). The police may only enter without a written order in cases of pressing necessity, for example to help someone or because of a fire (Law 1801 of 2016, art. 163).
  • The police do not decide who is right under the contract: their measures on occupation of a property are provisional and the merits are decided by a judge (Law 1801 of 2016, art. 80). You may record the procedure (art. 21).
  • If the host ends the contract, they must make an inventory of your belongings in front of witnesses before removing them (Commercial Code, art. 1198).

Where and how to complain

  1. Ask for the reason in writing and show your booking and payment. If the police arrive, ask for the officers’ names and badge numbers and the legal ground for their action.
  2. Alert the platform through its emergency channel and ask to be rebooked or refunded.
  3. Before leaving, photograph your belongings and leave no documents or money behind; if the host removes them, demand the inventory in front of witnesses.
  4. If there is violence or threats, or your belongings are kept, call 123 and report it.
  5. Then: written complaint to the host and the platform, and a complaint or lawsuit before the SIC.

Documents to gather

  • Booking and proof of payment
  • Messages from the host and the platform
  • Video or audio of what happened, with the officers’ names and badge numbers
  • Receipts for the new accommodation and transport
  • Photos or inventory of your belongings

Other situations

  • My booking was cancelled
  • I arrived at the hotel and they had no room for me (overbooking)
  • The place wasn’t as advertised
  • Extra charges or deposit not returned
  • Damage, accident or theft at the property
  • The travel agency or package didn’t deliver
  • I want to cancel my booking

If you are in danger, call 123 (Colombia’s emergency line).
