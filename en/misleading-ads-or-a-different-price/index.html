Misleading ads or a different price

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Complaint to the seller: 15 business days to reply.

What the law says

  • Misleading advertising is prohibited; the advertiser is liable for the harm it causes (Law 1480, art. 30).
  • You only have to pay the advertised price (Law 1480, art. 26).
  • Online offers must state how long they last and whether the product is available (Law 2439 of 2024).

Where and how to complain

  1. Keep the advert (a dated screenshot).
  2. Written complaint asking them to honour what was advertised.
  3. Report it to the SIC; if you suffered harm, file a claim.

Documents to gather

  • Screenshot of the advert
  • Invoice
  • Messages
