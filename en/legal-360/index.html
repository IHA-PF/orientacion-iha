How it works

1. Tell us what happened

We identify your situation, the people or companies involved and the outcome you are seeking.

2. Organise the information

We explain which documents are needed and how to gather them. Do not send sensitive files through unauthorised channels.

3. A legal professional reviews the case

A lawyer assesses the context, applicable rules and available options. Digital tools assist but do not replace professional judgment.

4. Agree on the service and follow-up

Before work begins, you receive the scope, fees, timelines and exclusions in writing.

Who is it for?

Guests and travellers

Cancelled bookings, accommodation that differs from the listing, charges, flights, agencies and platforms.

Hosts and property owners

RNT, tourist accommodation, condominium rules, listings, withheld payouts, suspended accounts and sanctions.

Consumers and businesses

Online purchases, warranties, advertising, contracts, personal data, fraud and platform relationships.

What is available now and what is still being developed

IHA already provides free guidance, information sheets, evidence guides and legal services with published scope and prices. LEGAL 360 organises that support into a coordinated route. The private self-service portal, unified digital case file and some automations are development stages; they are not presented as live features yet.

Transparency and professional oversight

  • Every service is confirmed in writing before it starts: scope, price, taxes, timelines and exclusions.
  • General guidance is not an individual legal opinion and does not by itself create a lawyer-client relationship.
  • Legal opinions and professional actions are reviewed and undertaken by lawyers authorised in Colombia.
  • No outcome is guaranteed: decisions also depend on companies, third parties and authorities.
  • Acceptance of a matter depends on professional competence, availability and conflict-of-interest checks.

Frequently asked questions

Is IHA LEGAL 360 software I can subscribe to today?

No. LEGAL 360 is the integrated legal-service model. Some functions are delivered through IHA's current services; the unified client portal and certain automations are still being developed.

Does artificial intelligence decide my case?

No. It may help organise information or prepare drafts, but legal analysis and decisions must be reviewed by a lawyer.

Does it automatically include a lawsuit or court representation?

No. Representation and each formal action are quoted, contracted and authorised separately, where appropriate.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the service and complexity. See the services and prices page; you will receive a written proposal before work begins.
