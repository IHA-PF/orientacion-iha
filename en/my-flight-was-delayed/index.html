Your consumer rights in Colombia › Transport

My flight was delayed

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Supertransporte states a 15-day reply period for complaints.

What the law says

  • More than 1 hour and less than 3, caused by the airline: a snack and a phone call.
  • More than 3 and less than 5 hours: meals as well, depending on the time of day.
  • More than 5 hours: additional compensation of at least 30% of the fare for that leg.
  • If the delay goes past 10 p.m.: accommodation (if you do not live in that city) and transfers.
  • For weather or other causes outside the airline's control there is no compensation, but you are entitled to information, rebooking or a refund.

Where and how to complain

  1. Ask at the counter for written proof of the delay.
  2. Written complaint to the airline or the agency.
  3. If they do not resolve it, complain to the Superintendencia de Transporte; the SIC is also competent on consumer protection.

Documents to gather

  • Ticket and boarding pass
  • Proof of the delay
  • Expense receipts
