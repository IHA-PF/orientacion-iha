Guest records: TRA and foreign guest reporting (SIRE)

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 19 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Report every foreign guest’s arrival and departure in SIRE within the deadline set by Migración Colombia.

What the law says

  • Every accommodation provider must record guests on the Accommodation Registration Card (TRA), which serves as proof of the lodging contract (Law 2068 of 2020, art. 22).
  • Foreign guests must also be reported to Migración Colombia through SIRE (Resolution 2357 of 2020), and failing to do so on time can lead to fines.
  • Accommodation providers must hold an insurance policy covering harm to guests and third parties (Law 2068 of 2020, art. 32).
  • In Medellín, the opening of a short-term rental must be notified in writing to the nearest police station (Law 1801 of 2016, art. 87).

Where and how to complain

  1. Complete the TRA for each guest on arrival.
  2. Report each foreign guest’s arrival and departure in SIRE.
  3. Keep proof of each record and a valid insurance policy.

Documents to gather

  • TRA records
  • SIRE reports
  • Liability insurance policy
