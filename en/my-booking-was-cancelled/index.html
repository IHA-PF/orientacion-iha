Your consumer rights in Colombia › Accommodation and tourism

My booking was cancelled

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Complain in writing straight away; the supplier has 15 business days to reply.
  • Claim before the SIC: up to one year.

What the law says

  • If the provider does not deliver what you booked, you choose: another service of the same quality, or a refund or compensation. If they cannot offer an equivalent, they must book one with a third party at their own cost (Law 300 of 1996, art. 63).
  • Overbooking is treated the same way (Law 300 of 1996, art. 64).
  • Cancelling a confirmed booking to resell it at a higher price is a breach that the SIC can sanction.
  • If you booked through a lodging app, also check what the platform's policy offers.

Where and how to complain

  1. Write to the host or hotel and to the platform asking for equivalent accommodation or a refund.
  2. If you pay for more expensive accommodation or extra transport, keep the invoices.
  3. If they do not resolve it, file a complaint or claim with the SIC.

Documents to gather

  • Booking confirmation and proof of payment
  • Dated cancellation messages
  • Screenshots of the listing
  • Invoices for the extra costs
