Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Internal appeal: check the deadline in the platform’s terms; it is usually short.
  • Personal data request: 10 business days, extendable by 5.

What the law says

  • The platform’s terms set its rules, an internal appeal and sometimes arbitration abroad. If the service is offered in Colombia, Colombian law and the SIC may also apply.
  • If you use the platform as a consumer, abusive clauses have no effect (Law 1480, arts. 42 and 43).
  • You can ask the platform what personal data it holds about you and what it uses it for: it must answer within 10 business days, extendable by 5 (Law 1581 of 2012, art. 14).

Where and how to complain

  1. Ask in writing for the specific reason and the rule applied.
  2. Use the internal appeal and keep proof.
  3. If it isn’t resolved, complain to the SIC or use the route set in the terms.

Documents to gather

  • Suspension notices and emails
  • Downloaded copy of your data
  • Screenshots of your profile and history
