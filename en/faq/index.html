How much does it cost?

The sheet and the first guidance are free. If your case needs a service, such as reviewing your file or preparing a claim, we tell you the price before starting and you decide.

Do you have a plan for hosts?

Yes. The Anfitrión en regla plan costs COP 979,000 a year per property, or COP 759,000 per property from 2 properties, VAT included: 12 months for the price of 11. You can also pay monthly (COP 89,000 or 69,000). It includes your obligations calendar with reminders, rule alerts, one written question a month and a yearly review. You can cancel at any time; if you paid for the year and cancel early, we refund what you paid minus the months started, at the monthly price.

How much do your services cost?

The guidance on the site is free. If you want a lawyer to take it on, each service has a fixed price including VAT: for example, a case review costs COP 179,000 and a documented complaint COP 449,000. Before we start, we send you in writing what is included.

How quickly do you reply?

We reply through the same channel you used, during Colombian business hours.

Do you work outside Medellín or with people abroad?

Yes. We work remotely, with people all over Colombia and with those living abroad.

I’m a foreigner. Does Colombian law protect me?

Yes. If you bought or received the service in Colombia, you have the same rights as a Colombian. We guide you in your language: Spanish, English, French or Portuguese.

Do I have to go to court?

In most cases, no. The first step is a written complaint to the company; if it doesn’t answer, you turn to the relevant authority, such as the SIC. Only some cases reach a judge, and we tell you beforehand.

Do you guarantee the outcome?

No. No lawyer can promise an outcome. We tell you clearly what is possible, what the risks are and what it costs.

What evidence do I need?

It depends on the case, but almost always: the listing or contract, proof of payment, messages with the company and dated photos. The evidence guide details it by type of case.

Evidence guide

Can you represent me against a multinational?

Yes. Besides the complaint and the SIC, with your written authorisation we can bring your case to the OECD National Contact Point, which offers free mediation with the company.

The OECD National Contact Point (NCP)

What do you do with my data?

This page doesn’t store your answers and counts visits anonymously, without cookies. If you send us your sheet, we use your data only to guide you and reply, and you can ask us to correct or delete it (Colombian Law 1581 of 2012).

Is the information on this page legal advice?

It is general guidance, with sources and an update date. It doesn’t replace the review of your case, which our lawyers carry out before taking any action.
