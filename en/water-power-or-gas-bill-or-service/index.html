Your rights and obligations in Colombia › Other purchases and services

Water, power or gas: bill or service

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Appeal for reconsideration: 5 days from when you learn of the decision.
  • Reply: 15 business days.
  • Bills older than 5 months can no longer be disputed.

What the law says

  • You can complain to the company; it must reply within 15 business days. If it does not reply, the complaint is deemed resolved in your favour (Law 142 of 1994, art. 158).
  • They cannot make you pay the disputed bill to process your appeal; pay the undisputed part or the average of the last five billing periods (art. 155).
  • They cannot suspend or cut off the service while they decide an appeal filed on time, with some exceptions (art. 155).
  • Bills issued more than five months ago can no longer be disputed (art. 154).

Where and how to complain

  1. Written complaint to the company.
  2. Appeal for reconsideration and, in the alternative, an appeal to the Superintendencia de Servicios Públicos.

Documents to gather

  • Bills
  • Photos of the meter
  • Complaint filing number
