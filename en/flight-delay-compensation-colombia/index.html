How much does the airline owe you for the delay?

For flights covered by Colombia’s aviation rules (RAC 3), such as domestic flights. Enter the price of the flight segment and how long it was delayed: we tell you what you are entitled to.

Work out what you are owed
DelayWhat you are entitled to
Under 1 hour
  • With a delay under 1 hour, the rules provide no assistance or compensation.
1 to 3 hours
  • A snack and a phone call, or the quickest means of communication.
3 to 5 hours
  • A snack and a phone call, or the quickest means of communication.
  • A meal depending on the time: breakfast, lunch or dinner.
More than 5 hours
  • A snack and a phone call, or the quickest means of communication.
  • A meal depending on the time: breakfast, lunch or dinner.
  • Compensation of at least 30% of the segment price, in cash, unless you accept another form of payment.

If the delay goes past 10 p.m. and you don’t live in that city: Accommodation and transfers between the airport and the hotel.

If the cause is outside the airline’s control, such as weather, there is no cash compensation, but you are owed information and, depending on the case, rebooking or a refund.

Source: Transport Superintendency, air sector FAQ (RAC 3). This is an estimate: the airline may dispute the cause of the delay.
