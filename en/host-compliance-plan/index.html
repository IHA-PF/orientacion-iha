Anfitrión en regla: your short-term rental kept compliant, every month

Our legal team tracks the deadlines and rules for your short-term rental, alerts you on WhatsApp in good time and answers when you have a question. You keep renting with peace of mind.

What’s included

  • Your obligations calendar, with a WhatsApp reminder before each date: RNT renewal (1 January to 31 March) and the FONTUR return every quarter.
  • An alert when a rule affecting your rental in your city changes, explained in a few lines with what you need to do.
  • One written question a month about your rental, answered by a lawyer within 2 business days.
  • A yearly compliance review: RNT, listing, building rules, guest reporting (TRA and SIRE) and house rules, with a list of what’s missing.
  • Priority if a problem comes up: a fine, a complaint from the building or a visit from the city.

Price

  • 1 property: COP 89,000 a month per property. VAT included.
  • 2 to 5 properties: COP 69,000 a month per property. VAT included. Plus: a 30-minute video call every quarter and a model management contract.
  • 6 properties or more: Custom. We set the scope and price together after a diagnosis.

Pay for the full year and you pay 10 months for 12.

No lock-in: you can cancel at any time, through the same channel you signed up with. Before we start, we send you the contract and payment details in writing.

What’s not included

  • Doing the filings for you (RNT, FONTUR, DIAN). If you need it, we quote it separately.
  • Tax returns: that is your accountant’s job.
  • Defending you in a sanction procedure or a lawsuit: quoted separately, before we start.

Why it’s worth it

  • If the RNT isn’t renewed by 31 March, the registration is automatically suspended from 1 April and you can’t operate legally.
  • Building fines repeat while the breach lasts and can add up to ten times the monthly condo fee (Law 675 of 2001, art. 59).
  • You know what you pay each month, with no surprises, and you have someone to ask before deciding.
