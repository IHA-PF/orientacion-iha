Anfitrión en regla: your short-term rental kept compliant, all year

Our legal team tracks the deadlines and rules for your short-term rental, alerts you on WhatsApp in good time and answers when you have a question. You keep renting with peace of mind.

What’s included

  • Your obligations calendar, with a WhatsApp reminder before each date: RNT renewal (1 January to 31 March), the FONTUR return every quarter and, once published, the dates of Medellín’s new zoning plan (POT).
  • An alert when a rule affecting your rental in your city changes, explained in a few lines with what you need to do.
  • One written question a month about your rental, answered by a lawyer within 2 business days, in one page at most. Unused questions don’t carry over.
  • A yearly compliance review: RNT, listing (including AI-written texts), building rules, owner’s authorisation, guest reporting (TRA and SIRE) and house rules, with a list of what’s missing. With yearly payment it’s done in your first month; with monthly payment, from the fourth month.
  • If a problem comes up (a fine, a complaint from the building or a visit from the city): a guidance call within 1 business day and a written action plan.

Price

  • 1 property: COP 979,000 a year per property. VAT included. One month free compared with paying monthly, which costs COP 89,000 a month. The host templates are included in the first year.
  • 2 to 5 properties: COP 759,000 a year per property. VAT included. One month free compared with paying monthly, which costs COP 69,000 a month. Plus: a 30-minute video call every quarter and a model management contract. The host templates are included in the first year.
  • 6 properties or more: Custom. We set the scope and price together after a diagnosis.

Pay for the year and get 12 months for the price of 11. If you prefer, you can also pay month by month.

You can cancel at any time, through the same channel you signed up with. If you cancel the yearly plan before it ends, we refund what you paid minus the months already started, charged at the monthly price. For example, if you paid for the year and cancel in the third month, we refund COP 712,000 (979,000 minus 3 × 89,000). The yearly plan doesn’t renew itself: we remind you a month before it ends. You have 5 business days to withdraw, unless you ask us to start sooner (Colombian Law 1480 of 2011, art. 47). Before we start, we send you the contract and payment details in writing.

What’s not included

  • Doing the filings for you (RNT, FONTUR, DIAN). If you need it, we quote it separately.
  • Tax returns: that is your accountant’s job.
  • Defending you in a sanction procedure, a police complaint procedure or a lawsuit: quoted separately, before we start.
  • Fines, penalties, fees and filing costs: the plan never pays or refunds them.

Why it’s worth it

  • If the RNT isn’t renewed by 31 March, the registration is automatically suspended from 1 April and you can’t operate legally.
  • Building fines repeat while the breach lasts and can add up to ten times the monthly condo fee (Law 675 of 2001, art. 59).
  • One payment a year, no surprises and no payment reminders, and someone to ask before deciding.
