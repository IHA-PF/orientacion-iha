Your rights and obligations in Colombia › Accommodation and tourism

The place wasn’t as advertised

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Report it in writing the same day: platforms often set short deadlines to report problems (check their terms).

What the law says

  • Information and advertising must be truthful; whoever advertises is liable for what they offer (Law 1480, arts. 23 and 30).
  • If the service does not match, you can ask for it to be provided as agreed or for a refund (service warranty, Decree 735 of 2013, art. 16).
  • If the platform lets a provider list without an active National Tourism Registry (RNT) number, the platform is liable to you for misleading advertising (Law 2068 of 2020, art. 39).

Where and how to complain

  1. When you arrive, take photos and video with the date visible.
  2. Write to the host and the platform asking for a fix, relocation or a refund.
  3. If they do not resolve it, file a complaint or claim with the SIC.

Documents to gather

  • Screenshots of the listing as it was when you booked
  • Photos and video of the place
  • Messages with the host and the platform
