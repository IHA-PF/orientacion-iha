Your rights and obligations in Colombia › Transport

Intercity bus: ticket, service or luggage

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Reply to requests: 15 days.
  • Consumer protection claim: 1 year.

What the law says

  • You have the right to information, to travel in quality conditions, to protection against misleading advertising, to carry your luggage and, under the rules, to withdraw from or give up the ticket (Supertransporte guide).
  • There are also rules for travelling with pets and with minors: ask the company before buying.
  • Refund percentages depend on the rules and on the company: ask for the policy in writing.

Where and how to complain

  1. Complaint to the company or to the intermediary that sold you the ticket.
  2. If they do not resolve it, Superintendencia de Transporte; then the courts or the SIC.

Documents to gather

  • Ticket
  • Photos
  • Receipts
  • Bus details and time
