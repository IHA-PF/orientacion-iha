I want to return or change a plane ticket

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Make the request in writing and before the flight.

What the law says

  • You can give up the trip before the flight; the airline may keep a limited part of the fare under the aviation rules.
  • Promotional fares may have different conditions: check the ones you accepted when buying.

Where and how to complain

  1. Request the withdrawal or cancellation in writing and keep proof.
  2. If they do not refund, complain and then go to the Superintendencia de Transporte or the SIC.

Documents to gather

  • Ticket and fare conditions
  • Proof of payment
  • Proof of the request
