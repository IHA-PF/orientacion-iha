Your rights and obligations in Colombia › Accommodation and tourism

Damage, accident or theft at the property

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Report it in writing to the provider the same day.

What the law says

  • You have the right to safety and not to suffer harm under normal conditions of use (Law 1480).
  • Tourist accommodation providers must have liability insurance for harm to guests and third parties, covering at least death, disability, injury, damage to third-party property and medical costs (Law 2068 of 2020, art. 32). Ask for the policy details.
  • If a crime occurred, the criminal complaint comes first; the consumer claim comes after.

Where and how to complain

  1. If there is danger, call 123.
  2. Get medical care and file a police report if relevant.
  3. Written report to the hotel or host and to the platform.
  4. Claim for the damage; if they do not reply, a civil claim or a claim before the SIC.

Documents to gather

  • Photos and video
  • Medical report
  • Police report
  • Messages with the provider
