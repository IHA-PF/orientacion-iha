Rules that apply to almost everything

  • Legal warranty: every product must work for what it is sold for. If no period is stated, a new product's warranty is one year from delivery (Law 1480, art. 8). A used item has a three-month warranty, unless it was sold without warranty and you accepted this expressly and in writing (Decree 735 of 2013, art. 12).
  • Free repair is the rule. If the fault comes back, you choose: another repair, a replacement or a refund (Law 1480, art. 11). Repairs must be done within 30 business days unless the SIC sets another period, and refunds within 15 business days after you return the item (Decree 735 of 2013, arts. 8 and 10).
  • Poorly provided services: you choose whether the service is provided again or your money is refunded (Decree 735 of 2013, art. 16).
  • Price: it must be stated in Colombian pesos, and you only have to pay the advertised price (Law 1480, art. 26).
  • Unfair terms, such as those limiting the supplier's liability, have no effect (Law 1480, arts. 42 and 43).
  • Minimum contract term: it is only valid if expressly agreed and, as a rule, cannot exceed one year (Law 1480, art. 41).
  • First complain in writing to the supplier; it must reply within 15 business days. Keep a copy: it is required before you can sue (Law 1480, art. 58, para. 5).
  • Claim before the SIC: no later than one year after the warranty expires (Law 1480, art. 58, para. 3). According to the SIC, if your claim is below 40 monthly minimum wages, you do not need a lawyer.
  • Online purchases: 5 business days to withdraw, refund within 15 calendar days at most, and payment reversal within 5 business days in the cases set by law (Law 1480 and Law 2439 of 2024).

Sources:

  • Ley 1480 de 2011, Estatuto del Consumidor
  • Decreto 735 de 2013, garantías
  • Ley 2439 de 2024, comercio electrónico (resumen Holland & Knight)
  • SIC: preguntas frecuentes, asuntos jurisdiccionales (2022)
