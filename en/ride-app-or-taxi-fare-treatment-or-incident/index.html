Your consumer rights in Colombia › Transport

Ride app or taxi: fare, treatment or incident

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Online payment reversal: 5 business days, in the cases set by law.

What the law says

  • Whoever charges you must state the price truthfully and handle your complaint (Law 1480).
  • Taxis and public transport: Superintendencia de Transporte and the city's mobility department.
  • If there was a safety incident, report it first (123).

Where and how to complain

  1. Complaint in the app with screenshots of the trip and the charge.
  2. If you paid online by card and it applies, ask for the reversal.
  3. Complaint to the SIC; for taxis and public service, Superintendencia de Transporte.

Documents to gather

  • Screenshots of the trip (route, driver, plate, price)
  • Statement showing the charge
  • Messages with support
