New vehicle with defects

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Repair: 30 business days unless the SIC sets another period (Decree 735 of 2013).
  • Claim: up to one year after the warranty expires.

What the law says

  • The vehicle has the warranty stated in its manual; if no period was stated, one year from delivery (Law 1480, art. 8).
  • Free repair of parts with manufacturing defects; if the fault comes back, you choose another repair, a replacement or a refund (Law 1480, art. 11).
  • Check whether your model has an open safety recall.

Where and how to complain

  1. Written complaint to the dealer or importer, with the service order.
  2. If the fault comes back, ask in writing for a replacement or a refund.
  3. Claim before the SIC.

Documents to gather

  • Invoice and warranty manual
  • Service orders for each workshop visit
  • Photos or video of the fault
