The travel agency or package didn’t deliver

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Reply to your complaint: 15 business days.
  • Claim before the SIC: up to one year.

What the law says

  • If a tourism service you bought is not provided, you choose another of the same quality, or a refund or compensation (Law 300 of 1996, art. 63).
  • Package information must be truthful, complete and given before you pay (Law 1480).
  • Complain to the agency and to the direct provider (hotel, airline, operator): both may be liable.

Where and how to complain

  1. Written complaint to the agency and the provider.
  2. If they do not resolve it, file a complaint or claim with the SIC.

Documents to gather

  • Package contract or voucher
  • Package advertising
  • Payments
  • Messages
