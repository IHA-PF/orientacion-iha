Your rights and obligations in Colombia › Transport

Lost, delayed or damaged baggage

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 19 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Damage, loss or theft: written complaint within 7 days at the latest.
  • Delay: complaint within 21 days of the date it should have arrived.

What the law says

  • If your baggage is delayed, the airline must cover basic items and compensate you under the aviation rules.
  • On international flights, the Montreal Convention limits compensation for baggage; declare valuables at check-in.

Where and how to complain

  1. Before leaving the airport, report it at the airline's desk and ask for the claim number.
  2. Written complaint within the deadline.
  3. If they do not resolve it, Superintendencia de Transporte.

Documents to gather

  • Ticket and baggage tag
  • Airport report
  • Photos of the damage
  • Receipts for emergency purchases
