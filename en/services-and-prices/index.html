Free help first; if you need it, a lawyer does it for you

The guides, the express route and the complaint letter are free. If you’d rather we handle it, each service has a fixed, published price, VAT included.

Before you pay

If the amount at stake is small, the free route is often enough: a direct complaint to the company and, if it doesn’t answer, SIC Facilita or the Superintendency. We’ll tell you frankly before charging you.

If you’re a host

Anfitrión en regla plan

COP 89,000 a month per property. Obligations calendar, rule alerts and one question a month to a lawyer. From COP 69,000 a month per property, VAT included.

Zoning plan (POT) transition audit

COP 690,000 per property, VAT included. COP 620,000 with the Anfitrión en regla plan; COP 390,000 for each extra property in the same building.

Includes:
