The guides, the express route and the complaint letter are free. If you’d rather we handle it, each service has a fixed, published price, VAT included.

Before you pay

If the amount at stake is small, the free route is often enough: a direct complaint to the company and, if it doesn’t answer, SIC Facilita or the Superintendency. We’ll tell you frankly before charging you.
