Does my area allow short-term rentals? (Medellín)

What the law says, the deadlines and where to complain. Then get free guidance in 2 minutes.

Reference translation of Colombian rules: the Spanish legal text prevails.

Information updated on 8 November 2026.

Deadlines that matter

  • Check the zoning before buying or listing.

What the law says

  • Besides the RNT, the area’s zoning must allow tourist accommodation under the land-use plan (POT).
  • In Medellín, under the current POT (Agreement 48 of 2014), short-term rentals are allowed in medium- and high-mixed-use zones.
  • A draft revision of the POT would limit it to high-mixed-use zones, special treatment zones and transit-oriented development areas; it has not been approved yet.

Where and how to complain

  1. Check your property’s zoning with Medellín’s Planning Department and ask for the written land-use statement.
  2. Check that the building rules authorise the activity.
  3. Follow the news on the new POT before investing.

Documents to gather

  • Land-use statement
  • Title deed and property certificate
  • Building rules
